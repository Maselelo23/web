Features

Everything the master spreadsheet did, and the parts it never could

Your register, your formulas and your targets — turned into screens your team can actually work in, with the checks and paperwork a commercial operation needs.

Animal register & profiles

  • Every animal with number, tag, breed, type, colour, gender, brand mark and holding
  • Automatic next available animal number when you add a new animal
  • Six-generation pedigree, up and down, downloadable as a PDF certificate
  • Photographs, comments and a full change history on each profile
  • Filter by farm-bred or bought, year of purchase, breeding readiness and more

Breeding, lambing & dam performance

  • Mating events with sire, method and expected birth date from your gestation setting
  • Kids and lambs recorded individually — gender, birth weight, survived or lost
  • Last date each dam gave birth, lambing interval and survival percentage
  • Dam rankings weighted the way you choose, so your best breeders are obvious
  • Alerts for overdue births and missed cycles

Health & biosecurity

  • Vaccine schedules with name, purpose, dosage and interval — due dates calculated for you
  • Vaccination history per animal, plus manual entries where needed
  • Sickness cases with symptoms, diagnosis, treatment, outcome and follow-up
  • Quarantine records and biosecurity incidents
  • Outbreak detection when the same symptoms cluster inside your chosen window

Sales, mortality & legal documents

  • Guided sale capture that only offers animals actually in stock
  • A VAT invoice and a government animal movement certificate generated and linked automatically
  • Documents carry your company name, registration, VAT number, logo and banking details
  • Numbered, stored permanently against the animal, viewable on screen and downloadable
  • Mortality capture with cause of death and photographs of the animal

Reports & analysis

  • Stock on hand by age, breed, type, gender and farm
  • Herd movement per year — born, bought, sold, died
  • Survival, lambing rate, mortality and margin against the targets you set
  • Top customers and supplier performance, rated on how their animals actually did
  • Click any figure to open the animals behind it; export anything to Excel

Stock control & approvals

  • Stock verification with date, the person who counted and their commentary
  • Two levels of access: level 1 captures, level 2 approves
  • Sales, mortality and stock adjustments only update the master once approved
  • Disposals handled as their own adjustment category
  • Every approval and rejection recorded with who and when

Your team

  • Invite your people by email with their name, phone and job title
  • Owner, farm manager, data capture, reporting and view-only roles
  • Password resets and removals handled by the owner
  • Support access, when you ask for it, is time-limited with a start and expiry

Your master file, kept

  • Your existing register was imported and remains the source of truth
  • Full-register Excel backup on demand, plus daily or weekly backup reminders
  • Nothing is thrown away — sold and deceased animals keep their history and parentage

Security & isolation

  • Each company is a separate tenant; no company can see another's records
  • Rules are enforced in the database itself, not just hidden in the screen
  • Invitation-only accounts — there is no public sign-up into the system
  • Photographs and documents are stored privately, never on a public link
  • An unchangeable security log of sensitive actions
