Common questions

Can I bring my existing spreadsheet across?

Yes. Your master register is imported, checked for duplicates and blanks, and becomes the live source of truth — parentage and history included.

Can other farms see my animals?

No. Each company's records are locked to that company in the database itself, not just hidden on screen.

How do my staff get accounts?

You invite them by email and choose their level. There is no public sign-up into the system.

Can I get my data out?

At any time — the full register exports to Excel, and documents download as PDFs to your computer.
